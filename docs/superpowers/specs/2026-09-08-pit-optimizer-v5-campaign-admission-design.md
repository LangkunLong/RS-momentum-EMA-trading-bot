# PIT optimizer V5 campaign admission and evaluated-round stopping

Principal design ruling for the authorized transition goal. Architectural scope: one optional operational policy, native request admission, and campaign boundary stopping. No paid run is authorized. Existing user constraints prohibit tests, synthetic evidence, historical mutation, credentials/provider/evaluation activity during development, and commits. Principal retains architectural decisions under the standing transition instruction.

## Problem and chosen approach

C-b is independently approved: H1–H3 source and both actual completed-history summaries pass. The existing launcher still limits lifecycle attempts without stopping at two evaluated rounds or retaining the reserve needed for that milestone. Raising the attempt/call caps alone does not implement the promised C-c stop policy. An observer exception, substituted dependency or manually reconstructed campaign driver would bypass the normal control contract.

Use a separate immutable campaign admission policy and enrollment binding. Preserve all existing canonical manifest, capabilities, config, launch, request, ledger and receipt shapes and identity formulas. Native operations, request factory and ledger enforce the policy; no wrapper dependency, callback cancellation or alternative executor is introduced. A standalone policy/compliance report may add information without changing existing summary fields or formulas.

Alternatives considered: a two-attempt run allows no rejection recovery and cannot stop early for an unattainable two-evaluated milestone; a larger cap without first-class stopping can fund unwanted later attempts; modifying old serialized dataclasses would invalidate original identities. The sidecar is the smallest approach that meets both control and compatibility requirements.

## Concrete C-c limits

The eventual real proposal will use target two distinct evaluated feedback rounds, maximum three lifecycle attempts, eight external role calls, 600000 total tokens, and 8192 maximum output tokens per role. The prospective total-token envelope per role is 75000. There are zero automatic retries and zero schema-repair calls. Search remains one hypothesis, one tunable axis, one variant and one discovery survivor per template, with full-source escape disabled and development semantics only. Use the fixed evaluator/profile/data/parent authorities approved by C-b.

Resources remain one evaluator, fixed profile CPU/memory/PID/output limits, 300-second role calls, 600-second quick evaluation, 1200-second discovery episodes, 7200-second rounds, 21600-second campaign work deadline, and 60-second owned cleanup. Original persisted deadlines cannot refresh. The proposed allowance can accommodate one two-call render rejection plus two three-call evaluated rounds in any order, conditional on genuine candidates, actual packet bounds and successful execution/critic/cleanup. It does not guarantee model success. Other unevaluated outcomes consume the same finite slack.

No dollar quote is established here. C-c may retain audited reported spend with no hard dollar ceiling, stating that explicitly. If a finite USD authority is used later, policy validation must additionally prove the analogous conservative monetary reserve from that manifest's authenticated price bounds; token arithmetic alone cannot establish it.

## New immutable contract and compatibility

Add a small module `core/pit_optimizer_v5/campaign_admission.py` with new frozen typed policy and binding dataclasses. The policy contains an explicit schema/revision, original manifest reference, original round-1 adapter-config reference, original owner digest, repository-root identity, target evaluated rounds, and prospective total-token envelope. The binding contains the policy reference and the same original manifest/config/owner/repository identities. Its single namespace is `campaign-admission`, keyed by manifest SHA-256. Do not add fields or defaults to any existing canonical dataclass, and do not change existing identity domain formulas.

Authenticate exact types, references and all bound original values. Validate a paid development manifest, zero retries/repairs, the ordinary three-role single-candidate lifecycle assumptions, positive exact integers, target within attempt cap, at least three calls per target, and total token capacity at least maximum calls times envelope. Output cap must fit inside the envelope. If maximum USD is finite, require its authenticated price authority and enough total USD for maximum calls times the worst per-envelope charge, computed conservatively from the greater input/output unit price. The policy can restrict admission only; it cannot expand any manifest capability.

No enrolled binding means the legacy path. An explicitly expected policy reference, however, requires matching enrollment when execution/history is expected to be launched. Missing policy bytes, missing value/index halves, malformed/corrupt entries, mismatching owner/config/root, or a different supplied policy are hard failures. Enumerate value/index unions when checking selected policy state so misplaced or orphan selected bindings cannot disappear. Historical readers always use `repair=False` and do not migrate or append.

## Preparation, enrollment and crash behavior

C-c may create the real fresh manifest/config and the policy artifact for review, but no campaign launch, paid reservation or provider call. Initial request preview uses the actual empty repository, authenticated baseline and normal request factory. Before enrollment, validate that exact preview against the explicitly authenticated prepared policy using the same pure envelope predicate. Do not persist it under `roles/`, fabricate future results, or call a round to obtain it.

Add an optional explicit `campaign_policy_ref` to the native campaign operation and its command/launcher plumbing. Under the existing campaign-launch transition, authenticate the expected policy and normal freshness, enroll the immutable binding, then publish the unchanged original launch record. The first adapter composition occurs before launch enrollment today; neither factory nor ledger may cache absence of policy at construction. They must discover/authenticate it at their actual admission call.

Resume always discovers existing enrollment; omitting the argument cannot disable it. A supplied reference must match. Do not enroll a previously launched legacy campaign. A binding-before-launch crash may continue only with the same explicit expected policy, original config/owner/root, and verified no-start/no-spend state. Never treat a contradictory launch, orphan authority or different policy as permission to replace records. Exact existing operational recovery semantics may finish an interrupted publication; no historical repair is permitted.

An optional sidecar cannot prove that every enrollment artifact was not deleted wholesale. The reviewed fresh launch/grant and final compliance invocation must independently pin the expected policy reference. When that expectation exists, absence is failure, never legacy mode. No new claim of atomic cross-file snapshots or hostile deletion recovery is made.

## Envelope admission and reserve invariant

One shared pure helper evaluates the exact ordinary `RoleRequestV5` with existing `prospective_role_usage_v5`: wire message bytes, response schema allowance, declared overhead, plus maximum output. Reject a total exceeding the policy envelope; never truncate the request, substitute capabilities or invent a denied terminal.

Call this helper through a private guarded-return method at all three normal `LocalRoleRequestFactoryV5` build sites, after constructing the actual complete request and before returning it to runtime. Runtime calls the factory before `_complete_role` persists a request, so a refusal follows the existing generic stage-failure/cleanup path without a journal role completion or dangling request. Preserve its honest existing failure classification. No new fake zero-cost role package is needed.

Also enforce the identical predicate natively in `LocalRoleAuthorizationLedgerV5.reserve_role_slot`, inside the existing transition and before reservation append. Reload/authenticate enrollment and original launch, and recover the exact persisted request/call using immutable readers. Preserve all current request/model/schema/output/total-token/USD/owner/deadline checks and accounting order. Lower-level callers that bypass the factory cannot spend beyond the envelope; if they leave unsupported unjournaled requests, historical verification continues to reject them.

For conforming settlements each charged call consumes at most one envelope. With token cap at least call cap times envelope, every remaining authorized call therefore has an envelope available. Existing uncertain/unreported settlement remains conservative. An actual provider overage keeps its recorded classification and stops under the current failure rules; historical reporting must not erase that failure or demand that a nonconforming response satisfy a conforming-response invariant.

For enrolled execution, both native guards additionally require the exact matching persisted round configuration and started authority, original launch/config/owner/root, and unexpired original deadline. Direct native round entry can only continue an authenticated admission; it cannot authorize another attempt. Reconstruct the admitted round's logical prior-round prefix and apply the same target/reserve predicate before its first investigator call and any lower-level paid reservation. Author/critic continuation uses that original prior-round admission and does not subtract current-round usage as if admitting another three-call round. Reject absent or contradictory authority before persistence/reservation respectively. This check must also reject a later round after the prior logical prefix already reached the goal. The explicitly authenticated, nonpersisting preparation preview is the sole pre-start exception; it invokes the pure envelope predicate separately and never enters live guards.

At a settled campaign boundary let `M = max(0, target - authenticated_evaluated_feedback_rounds)`. If M is zero, stop successfully before writing another round config/start/request. Otherwise require at least M remaining lifecycle slots, 3M remaining calls and 3M envelopes of remaining tokens. If a USD cap exists, also require 3M worst-envelope charges of remaining USD. Failure of any reserve condition stops without a new request or paid call. This invariant supersedes the earlier design note's suggestion of dynamic stage-by-stage future reserve state; no such extra state is required.

## Native campaign boundaries and failure precedence

Integrate in `_run_campaign_v5` using authenticated committed records, critics, checkpoint and successful cleanup evidence, with unchanged summary count formulas. Count distinct evaluated rounds, not variants, emitted rows, raw experiment totals or outer `completed` status. Authenticate actual usage and original launch/deadline before admission. Apply the same policy to reused and newly completed rounds; the existing reused-round shortcut must not skip it.

Reconciliation and ownership/integrity checks precede the success decision. Never claim success while an observed started round is unsettled or cleanup is missing/failed. Existing runtime/provider/cleanup/authority/deadline/novelty-exhaustion failures retain precedence; policy stopping cannot convert them into success or authorize another call. Contradictory policy admissions fail closed and cannot use callback results as authority. Normal crash recovery retains its original identities and deadlines.

Expose distinct operational reasons for evaluated target reached and insufficient milestone reserve without changing existing `OptimizerSummaryV5` fields. A policy stop is not a synthetic round outcome: do not append invented role, evaluation, cleanup or finalizer records. Repeated entry recomputes the same decision from durable evidence and cannot refresh capacities. No success beyond actual evidence is promised.

## Historical verification and scope

Add optional, strictly immutable policy compliance verification to the completed-history path. Original unenrolled archives must retain all returned fields and counts. For enrolled campaigns authenticate binding, policy and original launch/config/root/owner; validate every actual persisted paid request's prospective envelope; authenticate actual ledger facts using the approved shared reader; and verify each fresh-round admission against the previously completed evaluated rounds, spent calls/tokens, remaining attempts and original deadline. Use actual record/cleanup/journal/receipt ordering, including pre-start state, rather than a final counter alone. Reject a later admitted round after success or after reserve became insufficient.

The historical claim is authenticated logical-prefix eligibility plus persisted launch/start epoch checks against the original deadline. Existing per-round event sequences, cleanup and checkpoint bytes have no cross-round publication timestamp/link: compliance cannot prove those bytes existed before the next configuration/start publication. Do not infer a global timeline or add a durable witness in this change. Unstarted configuration records are observed pre-start state, not paid-execution evidence; completed-history verification rejects unfinished publication. Live operations enforce settled cleanup before writing/starting the next configuration, while historical reports explicitly retain this temporal-evidence limitation.

Expose a separate explicit-expected-policy compliance entry/report for the future launch and final evidence. Its caller-supplied expected reference is only a pin to authenticate, not a replacement authority. The public completed-history reader still authenticates its own original provenance; private composition may reuse already authenticated data-only facts. No operational adapter, nonce, recovery, repair, wrapper or callback enters this read path. Old canonical artifacts and summaries remain unchanged.

Keep exact preimages/diffs, protected-definition comparisons, explicit read/write/repair callgraphs, full evaluator map and complete actual archive hashes. No tests or synthetic cases may be read, created or run. Use scoped Ruff, in-memory compile and source/AST/hash inspection only until independent source review authorizes real immutable checks.

Do not add a policy to an old archive to exercise the new branch. Policy-positive behavior remains static until the real new C-c preparation/authorized campaign supplies actual evidence. Update readiness with this additional host change before final proposal preparation.

Then create the concrete real campaign package and initial packet, independently review its exact launch/budget/policy/source/environment identities, and ask for the separate paid grant as the final step. No commitment, external message, provider call, new evaluation or campaign execution is part of this design approval.
