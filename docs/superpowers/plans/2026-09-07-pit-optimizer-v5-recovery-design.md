Architecture decisions:

1. Expose a small explicit allowlist of existing authenticated entry-funnel integer counts to investigator. Append new evaluator evidence after headline metrics and before archive/history evidence, matching the required citation order. Future request IDs may change; historical requests remain unchanged. Omit unavailable counts. Counts overlap and do not establish causality.

Capture only that known ValueError at the candidate bundle-construction boundary; unknown errors, parent authority, collisions, deadlines and cancellation remain fatal. Do not construct valid source or policy identity for rejected bytes.
3. Add a dedicated render_rejected event binding existing PreValidationInvalidExperimentIdentityV5, assignment, accepted author artifact reference and closed rejection code. Do not relax validation event ordering. Append/replay authenticates author/template/hypothesis and recomputes identity and bounded assignment membership.
4. A rejected identity is terminal: no later render, validation, evaluation or critic evidence. Reuse ExperimentRecordV5(status=invalid), with all downstream evidence and policy revision absent. Check semantic linkage to the rejection authority at repository append/load, not only reference hashes.
5. Keep rejected candidates separate from materialized CandidateEvidenceV5. Publish legitimate all-invalid records/checkpoint without fabricating a critic. Such a completed lifecycle round is not an evaluated round.
6. The next investigator receives bounded rejection metrics from authenticated records. Recovery reuses persisted author/rejection evidence without repeating paid calls or refreshing deadlines. Historical terminal06 stays unchanged.

The design avoids evaluator-covered candidate_ir.py changes. Verified current host-only files include production_runtime.py, provider.py, rendering.py, runtime.py, memory.py, artifacts.py and summary.py. Compute the full evaluator identity before/after changes; do not assume closure membership stays fixed.
